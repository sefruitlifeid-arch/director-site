    What it does on the day

    A shot list a director would write

    Each shot gets a size, angle, lens, move and gear, plus one cinematic technique: foreground framing, silhouette, rack focus, worm's-eye. No two shots repeat the same size and angle.

    Choose voice-over or dialog and every shot carries its line.

    Boards with the same faces

    Director lays out each frame in 3D first: where the camera stands, where people stand and which way they face. The final drawing follows that layout, with your characters kept the same from panel to panel.

    A guide on the camera

    The panel floats over the live view, small, large or see-through. On-device vision checks the frame against it and gives one cue at a time: hold the phone level, step back, lower the camera, the light is behind them.
